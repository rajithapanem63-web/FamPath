AP EAPCET Counselling Guide 2024 (Synthetic Development Data)

OVERVIEW
AP EAPCET (Andhra Pradesh Engineering, Agriculture and Pharmacy Common Entrance Test) is the state-level entrance examination for admission to engineering, agriculture, and pharmacy courses in Andhra Pradesh.

ELIGIBILITY
- Students must have passed 10+2 (Intermediate) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
- Minimum 45% marks in PCM for general category; 40% for SC/ST.
- Must be a citizen of India and satisfy domicile requirements for AP state quota.

SEAT CATEGORIES
- State Quota: 85% of total seats are allocated to AP state candidates.
- Management Quota: 15% of total seats.
- NRI Quota: Separate allocation in private colleges.

RANK CATEGORIES
- OC: Open Category
- BC-A, BC-B, BC-C, BC-D, BC-E: Backward Classes sub-categories
- SC: Scheduled Castes
- ST: Scheduled Tribes
- EWS: Economically Weaker Sections (10% reservation)

COUNSELLING ROUNDS (Synthetic 2024 Dates)
- Round 1 Web Options Entry: July 15–20, 2024
- Round 1 Allotment: July 25, 2024
- Round 2 Web Options Entry: August 1–5, 2024
- Round 2 Allotment: August 10, 2024
- Round 3 (Spot Admission): August 20–25, 2024

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED (General)
- AP EAPCET Hall Ticket and Rank Card
- 10th (SSC) Marks Memo and Certificate
- 12th (Intermediate) Marks Memo
- Study/Residence Certificate (to prove AP domicile)
- Transfer Certificate (TC)
- Conduct Certificate
- Passport-size photographs (6 copies)
- Aadhaar Card

ADDITIONAL DOCUMENTS FOR RESERVED CATEGORIES
- Caste Certificate (BC/SC/ST)
- Income Certificate (for EWS and fee reimbursement)
- BC/SC/ST Validity Certificate

FEE REIMBURSEMENT
- AP Government provides fee reimbursement for students with family income below ₹2.5 lakhs per annum in government colleges.
- Students must apply for fee reimbursement through the AP government portal.
